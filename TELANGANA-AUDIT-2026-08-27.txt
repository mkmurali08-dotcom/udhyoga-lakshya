Primary official sources checked:
1. TGPSC current notifications/direct recruitment
2. Telangana Police Recruitment Board (TGPRB)
3. Medical & Health Services Recruitment Board (MHSRB)
4. Telangana district government recruitment pages (verified example: Hyderabad District)

Rules:
- Never label a closed application as OPEN.
- Never copy a job into unrelated categories.
- Official recruiting authority is the final source.
- Current-stage updates (merit list, applicant list, certificate verification, result, hall-ticket etc.) are labelled CURRENT STAGE.
- District recruitments are included only when a government district portal provides the notice.
- This page is an audit snapshot; future changes require a fresh check.
